# The World's News in One Picture — Wednesday 18 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118): sum up 18 November 2026's world news in one image.

A single front page that sums up one full day of world news — Wednesday 18 November 2026: **40 stories, six continents, one page.**

## What the page shows

- **9 illustrated panels**: Iran says drones targeted a US radar in Bahrain; Quad leaders meet in Manila; Xi and Putin defend open trade at APEC; Ukraine strikes the Kerch bridge for a second day; US officials say recent outages show no sign of cyberattack; Modi proposes free e-visas for ASEAN visitors; Cyclone Akvan prompts coastal warnings; Dhoni retires from his IPL playing career; Canada tests its Alert Ready emergency notifications.
- **A "Where the news happened" map** with the day pinned.
- **31 more stories** (numbered 10–40), all distinct from the panels: SpiceJet's Mumbai cancellations, Delhi Police and the reel-makers, India's Panipat SAF plant, the Uttarakhand bus crash, Nvidia's $115.8bn quarter, China–Canada tariff talks, UK sanctions on West Bank settlers, the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, Estonia's border closure with Russia, the lost US measles status, the Knesset Kahana bill, RAYE's 11 Grammy nods, the cyberattack on 178 big companies, Guinea's gold-mining crackdown, the Waymo freeway ban, Earthshot in Mumbai, India's 7.5% GDP growth and more.

## How it was made

The front-page artwork was created by the entrant. It was then repaired and completed in-session with Muse (`build/repair.py`, source art in `assets/source-frontpage.png`):

- the generated "31 More Stories" box padded the count — about a third of its items repeated a panel story or were vague filler (Putin–Xi bilateral, ASEAN maritime cooperation, cyclone preparedness, Canada's alert timing, the Shenzhen APEC forum, CSK plans). The whole box was redrawn with distinct, verified stories from the day's record;
- the page was fitted to the 1024×1024 / 1 MiB gate.
